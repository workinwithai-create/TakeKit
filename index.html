3 · How it judges (smart defaults, all editable)

Pitch = cents off the nearest note, plus notes outside the key or chord. Timing = note starts against the 8th grid (16th for MIDI). Level = against the other takes in that bar, and clipping. Dropouts = missing words or notes.

Pro vocal chain (comp playback, mic monitor, exports)

Order: noise gate (input) → high-pass → subtractive EQ → de-esser → fast + levelling compressor → tone EQ → saturation → output trim, with reverb and delay sends. Same graph live and in the offline export, with nothing random in it.

5 · Mix

Every chair → master → safety limiter. Exports skip the limiter and are peak-normalised to −1 dBFS.

6 · Export

48 kHz / 24-bit stereo at exactly your bar length with the downbeat on sample 0. It drops onto your DAW grid and loops without clicks. The MIDI has tempo, 4/4, a track per chair, every MIDI take, and markers naming the take used in each segment. Files are named takekit-<band>-<bpm>bpm-<key>.
