# TakeKit 20-second demo (captions only, no voiceover)

File: `demo/takekit-demo-20s.mp4`: 1280×720, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo, 20.87 s.
- Picture: a real Playwright screen capture of TakeKit (local build of the deployed code).
- Sound: TakeKit's own WAV export of the same session (`takekit-pop-92bpm-Am.wav`, 8 bars at 92 BPM, the comp over the Pop band), with a 0.6 s fade at the end.

| Time | Screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.9 | Song setup, Play pressed, band looping | Set your BPM, key and chords. A real-sample band loops. |
| 3.9–8.8 | Takes grid with per-bar scores and reasons | Sing pass after pass. Every bar of every take is scored: pitch, timing, level, dropouts. |
| 8.8–13.4 | Comp row; bar 1 clicked to lock, then unlocked | Auto-comp picks the best bar from each take. Click any cell to lock your own pick. |
| 13.4–17.1 | A, then B pressed | A/B: best whole take vs the comp, at matched loudness. |
| 17.1–20.9 | Export panel, Export WAV | Export 48 kHz/24-bit WAV, MIDI and stems. takekit.vercel.app · $24 once |

A caption stays on screen throughout: "Demo vocal: real CC0 recording (“Female-vibrato-1”, Beetricks, Wikimedia Commons). Band + mix: TakeKit's own WAV export." (v2, 2026-10-28; v1 used the FluidR3 Voice Oohs sample.)
