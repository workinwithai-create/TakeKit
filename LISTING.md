# TakeKit: store listing draft (not published)

**Title:** TakeKit: auto vocal comping in your browser
**Price:** $24 one-time (Lemon Squeezy license key)

**Short:** Sing pass after pass over your loop. TakeKit scores every bar of every take for pitch, timing, level and dropouts, then builds the best comp with the seams hidden in the breaths.

**Body:**
Comping vocals eats more studio time than sound design. TakeKit does the first pass for you:
- Loop your chords on a real-sample band (piano, finger bass, strings, acoustic kit) at your BPM and key.
- Press Record and keep singing. Every pass is a take. A MIDI keyboard records note takes at the same time.
- Every bar of every take gets a score and a reason ("+40¢ sharp", "late 124 ms", "dropout", "clipped").
- Auto-comp picks the best bar from each take, but keeps whole passes where it can. Seams move to the quietest point near the bar line with an equal-power crossfade.
- Click any cell to lock your own pick. A/B the best whole take against the comp at matched loudness.
- Export a 48 kHz / 24-bit WAV that drops onto your DAW grid, MIDI with a marker naming the take for each bar, and (unlocked) a stems ZIP with every raw take.

**Free:** up to 8-bar loops, WAV + MIDI export, 6 takes per lane.
**Unlock ($24 once):** 16-bar exports, the stems ZIP with every raw take, unlimited takes.

Works in Chrome/Edge on desktop with any audio interface and MIDI keyboard. On iPhone and iPad, Safari has no Web MIDI and no output selection (on-screen keys still work). iOS was checked in an emulator only, not on a real device.
Sounds: FluidR3 GM (MIT) and Virtuosity Drums (CC0). Nothing is uploaded: your takes stay in your browser.
